## Moral Media 2024 – April

We are excited to announce the seventh meeting of the Moral Media working group. This year’s #moralmedia24 agenda combines research proposals, a poster session, and breakout discussions sessions with the goal of generating multi-lab collaborations. Researchers working in the area of media and morality (broadly defined) are encouraged to attend. Please [register](https://osu.az1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6GAKUgtIXiNmc18) your attendance by February 14, 2024.

Support for #moralmedia24 is generously provided by The [School of Communication](https://comm.osu.edu/) at The Ohio State University.

Link to OSF: <https://osf.io/5vhau/>
